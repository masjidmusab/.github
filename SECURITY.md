# Security Policy

**Masjid Musab bin Umair (West Forsyth Islamic Center)** takes the security of our digital platforms, donor links, and community data seriously. We appreciate the efforts of security researchers and community volunteers who help keep our systems safe.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do NOT report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.**

If you discover a security vulnerability, exposed credential/API key, or misconfiguration in any `masjidmusab` repository or web property:

1. **Email us privately at:** **[security@masjidmusab.com](mailto:security@masjidmusab.com)**
2. **Include in your report:**
   * The repository name or affected URL (e.g., `masjidmusab.com` or `github.com/masjidmusab/website`)
   * A clear description of the vulnerability and potential impact
   * Step-by-step instructions or a proof-of-concept to reproduce the issue
   * Any suggested remediation, if known

---

## What to Expect

* **Acknowledgment:** You will receive a response from our IT security team within **72 hours** confirming receipt of your report.
* **Investigation & Fix:** We will validate the issue, prioritize remediation based on severity, and keep you updated on our progress.
* **Coordinated Disclosure:** Please allow our volunteer team reasonable time to deploy a fix before disclosing any details publicly.

---

## Scope & Safe Harbor

* **In Scope:** `masjidmusab.com`, subdomains, and public repositories under `github.com/masjidmusab`.
* **Out of Scope:** Third-party payment processors (e.g. PayPal) and external providers—please report vulnerabilities in those services directly to their respective security teams.
* **Good-Faith Testing:** We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith, avoid accessing or modifying community/donor data, and do not degrade service availability (no DDoS or automated stress testing).
