## 🤝 How to Contribute

We welcome designers, developers, and testers of all skill levels to contribute as *Sadaqah Jariyah*:

1. **Read our standards:** Review our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/masjidmusab/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/masjidmusab/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. **Pick an issue:** Browse open issues labeled `good first issue` or `help wanted` in [`musab-connect`](https://github.com/masjidmusab/website/issues).
3. **Comment before coding:** Leave a short note on the issue so our tech leads can assign it to you and avoid duplicate effort.
4. **Submit a Pull Request:** Follow the checklist in our PR template and request a review.

## 🔒 Security & Contact

* **Security Vulnerabilities:** Please do **not** open public GitHub issues for security flaws or exposed credentials. Follow our [Security Policy](https://github.com/masjidmusab/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) to report issues privately.
* **Inquiries:** Reach our technology team at [tech@masjidmusab.com](mailto:tech@masjidmusab.com) (for general inquiries [info@masjidmusab.com](mailto:info@masjidmusab.com)).
